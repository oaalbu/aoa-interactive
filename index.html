INTERACTIVE LIGHT

Light Pong

OPEN PROJECT +

A projected game played with light. Two players move their paddles with handheld light while a physical object becomes part of the playfield. Presented at Fête des Lumières in Lyon and Spotlight in Bucharest.

Octavian's contribution: concept, system architecture and sound design. Created with a French-German-Romanian team.

AUDIOVISUAL INSTALLATION

Vague News

OPEN PROJECT +

An interactive audiovisual puzzle about political communication and a saturated media environment. Participants use a modular pad to assemble video fragments drawn from political statements.

Artist: Octavian Albu. Presented within Organic Proxy at MNAC, and later at AMURAL V, NAG and BINAR.

INTERACTIVE PERFORMANCE

BREATH

OPEN PROJECT +

Dance, light, sound and interactive technology come together in a collaborative performance presented at the Goethe-Institut during Fête des Lumières. Movement data and sound from an inflatable structure shaped an evolving sound environment.

CINÉTic team: Octavian Albu, Maria Năstase, Emil Drăgan, Sebastian Comănescu, Nicolaie Prodromou and Sergiu Diță. Choreography: Natacha Paquignon. Inflatable: Frank Fierke.

PARTICIPATORY PERFORMANCE

Reconnect

OPEN PROJECT +

Inspired by the physical and participatory intensity of punk concerts, Reconnect explores how simple technology can invite an audience to become an active part of a collective performance.

Artist: Octavian Albu. Presented at Agora Digitalis / The Wild State during Ars Electronica 2020.

GROUP EXHIBITION

A collaborative exhibition of digital and interactive works addressing the Anthropocene and the relationship between humans, technology and the planet.
