Light Pong

A projected game controlled with light. Two players use a phone flashlight to move their paddles, while a physical object within the playfield becomes part of the game. Presented at Fête des Lumières in Lyon and Spotlight in Bucharest.

Original team member: concept development, system architecture and sound design. Created through a collaboration between participants from Romania, Germany and France.

Proposal · Code:ART 2027

LightOn Plaza

A proposed adaptation of Light Pong for Lytton Plaza, Palo Alto. A sculptural disc inspired by redwood growth rings would become an obstacle in the projected playfield, responding to each impact with light and sound.

The Tree Ring and the Lytton Plaza presentation are a concept for a future installation; the adaptation and collaborator participation remain subject to agreement.

About

Octavian Albu is a composer and sound designer based in Bucharest. His practice brings together sound, performance and interactive audiovisual work. He studied Interactive Technologies for Performing and Media Arts at UNATC from 2018 to 2020.
