INTERACTIVE ART · SOUND · PERFORMANCE

Octavian Albu.

BIO

Octavian Albu is a Bucharest-based artist, composer and sound designer working across interactive installations, performance and audiovisual media. His work uses sound, movement and simple interfaces to draw people into shared experiences that can be both playful and subversive, often engaging with political and social themes.

Drawing on a background in punk, electronic music and film, he brings together composition, sound design and interaction design. His work has been presented at Ars Electronica, Fête des Lumières and MNAC. He was nominated for a Gopo Award for Best Original Music in 2022.

Works:

01 / PARTICIPATORY PERFORMANCE · 2020

Reconnect

The audience as a musical instrument.

Reconnect investigates how simple technology can invite people into a collective performance. Inspired by the physical and participatory intensity of punk concerts, it considers the audience as an active part of the event. The research explored camera-based movement detection and a system that translated vocal pitch into notes for a digital instrument.

Presented at Agora Digitalis / The Wild State during Ars Electronica 2020.

02 / INTERACTIVE PERFORMANCE · 2019

BREATH

Dance, light and sound in a shared space.

BREATH brings dance, light, sound and interactive technology together through a French-German-Romanian collaboration. The sound-and-dance setup used wearable movement data and a microphone attached to an inflatable structure to shape sound in real time.

Presented at the Goethe-Institut during Fête des Lumières, Lyon.

03 / INTERACTIVE INSTALLATION · 2018–2019

Light Pong

A familiar game, played together through your phone’s flashlight.

A site-specific version of Pong where two participants control projected paddles with the light from their phones. A sculptural element becomes part of the playfield, turning a familiar game into a shared physical interaction with architecture.

Presented at Fête des Lumières, Lyon, and Spotlight, Bucharest.

04 / INTERACTIVE AUDIOVISUAL INSTALLATION · 2019

Vague News

A political puzzle that puts the viewer back in control, turning the TV remote into a tool of empowerment.

Vague News is an interactive audiovisual puzzle about political communication and the difficulty of navigating a saturated media environment. Participants use a modular pad to assemble a video puzzle built from political statements, similar to the Memory Game.

Exhibited as part of Organic Proxy at MNAC, Bucharest, and at AMURAL V, NAG and BINAR.

05 / INTERACTIVE SOUND INSTALLATION · 2020

Feedbacker

A sonic encounter with environmental damage and corporate responsibility.

Feedbacker uses recordings of natural environments and guitar effects pedals to explore the disproportionate environmental impact of individuals and major global companies. Visitors encounter the sounds and alter them through the installation’s controls.

Exhibition: BIOS.
